# Farmer guide (Uzhavan)

The farmer side of the app has four tabs: **Home**, **My Crops**, **Requests** and **Profile**.

## Sign in or create an account

Choose **Farmer** when you create an account. If you sign into the wrong app, you will be told which one to use.

## List a crop

1. Open **My Crops** and add a new listing.
2. Enter the crop, price per kilogram, expected quantity, minimum order and harvest date.
3. Save. Buyers can now find it.

You can edit a listing later. A listing with sales is hidden rather than deleted, so order history stays correct.

## Answer a request

New requests appear in **Requests**, and you get a notification. Open one to see the buyer, the quantity and the estimated value.

- **Accept** and set your final price. You can keep the listed price or change it.
- **Decline** and give a short reason. The buyer sees it.

You can only accept if enough of the crop is still unreserved.

## What happens next

When the buyer confirms, an order is created, the quantity is reserved, and you get a notification. The buyer then pays and books a truck. The driver collects the crop from your farm.

## Your money

The **Your money** screen lists each payment with its stage and due time. Your payout is exactly the price you accepted. Farmers are never charged a fee. The platform fee is added on top and paid by the buyer.

When you are paid depends on the setting your admin chose:

- **Advance on loading:** part is released when the driver confirms the crop is loaded, and the rest after delivery.
- **After delivery:** nothing is paid until the crop is delivered.

A farmer's advance is released by the driver confirming the load, not by the farmer saying it shipped.

## See what buyers want

**What buyers want** shows demand near you by category: volume, average price and how often farmers accept. It never shows an individual buyer's request.

## Get verified (free)

In **Profile**, open **Verification**. Submit your farmer card number with the farmer card and a land record. Staff review it, and you get a verified badge. Buyers can filter for verified farms.

## Chat and notifications

Buyers can message your farm. Open **Messages** in **Profile** to reply. The bell on the home screen shows new requests and updates. See [Messages, notifications and the assistant](messages-notifications-assistant.md).

## Get help

In **Profile**, tap **Help & support** to raise an issue or contact support.
