# Farmer guide (Uzhavan)

The farmer side of the app has four tabs: **Home**, **My Crops**, **Requests** and **Profile**.

## Sign in or create an account

Choose **Farmer** when you create an account. If you sign into the wrong app, you are told which one to use.

## Your home screen

**Home** shows your farm, your number of listed crops, how many are **Awaiting buyer**, your **Orders** and **Sales value**, and your **Recent orders**. It also has shortcuts to **List a new crop**, **Your money** and **What buyers want**.

## List a crop

1. Tap **List a new crop** on **Home**, or add one from **My Crops**.
2. Fill in the **New listing** form: crop name, category, grade, **Expected quantity (kg)**, **Minimum order (kg)**, **Your price (₹ per kg)**, **Harvest / pickup date**, and a description under **About this crop**.
3. Tap **List this crop**. Buyers can now find it.

To change a listing, open it from **My Crops**, edit, and tap **Save changes**. A listing that has sales is hidden (shown as **Unlisted**) rather than deleted, so order history stays correct.

## Answer a request

New requests appear in **Requests**, and you get a notification. Tap one to see the buyer, the requested quantity and the estimated value.

- **Accept:** the price field shows your listed price. Change **Price per kg (₹)** if you want a different final price, then tap **Accept at ₹...**.
- **Decline:** tap **Decline request**, optionally write a **Reason (optional)**, and tap **Send decline**. The buyer sees the reason.

You can only accept if enough of the crop is still unreserved.

## What happens next

When the buyer confirms, an order is created and the quantity is reserved. You get a notification. The buyer then pays and books a truck, and a driver collects the crop from your farm.

## Your money

Open **Your money** from **Home**. The first time, it asks **Where should we pay you?**. Enter the name on the account, account number and IFSC code, then tap **Save bank details**.

After that, each payment shows its stage, such as **Sent to your bank**. Your payout is exactly the price you accepted. Farmers are never charged a fee. The platform fee is added on top and paid by the buyer.

When you are paid depends on the admin's setting. With an advance, you get a percentage as soon as the driver picks up your crop, and the rest some hours after delivery. The screen shows the exact percentage and hours. The advance is released when the driver confirms the load, not when you say it shipped.

## See what buyers want

Tap **What buyers want** on **Home** to see demand near you by category: volume, average price, and how often farmers accept. It never shows an individual buyer's request.

## Get verified (free)

In **Profile**, open **Verification**. Submit your farmer card number with the farmer card and a land record. Staff review it, and you get a verified badge. Buyers can filter for verified farms.

## Chat and notifications

Buyers can message your farm. Open **Profile → Messages** to reply. The bell on the home screen shows new requests and updates. See [Messages, notifications and the assistant](messages-notifications-assistant.md).

## Get help

In **Profile**, tap **Help & support** to raise an issue or contact support. Tap **Ask a question** for quick answers.
