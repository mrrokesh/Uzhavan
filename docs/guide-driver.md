# Driver guide (Uzhavan)

The driver side of the app has three tabs: **Jobs**, **My Trips** and **Profile**.

## Create your account and truck

Choose **Driver** when you create an account. You enter your **Number plate** and **Load capacity (tons)** there, so the app knows which loads your truck can carry.

In **Profile**, tap **Edit fare** to set your **Base fare (₹)** and **Typical ETA (minutes)**. Buyers see these when choosing a truck.

## Go online

On **Jobs**, switch the toggle so it reads **Online**. Only online drivers appear when a buyer is picking a truck. Switch it back to **Offline** when you are done.

## Take a job

Jobs are paid bookings that were routed to your truck. Each card shows the pickup farm, the destination and the load. Tap **Accept trip** to take it. Another driver cannot take your job.

## Run the trip

Open the trip from **My Trips**. One button at the bottom moves it forward, and it changes after each step:

| Tap this | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept this trip** | You are taking the job (the same as **Accept trip** on the job card) |
| **I've reached the farm** | You are at the farm |
| **Crop is loaded** | The crop is on your truck |
| **Start the delivery** | You are driving to the warehouse |
| **Mark as delivered** | You have handed the crop over |

You cannot skip a step. Only tap **Crop is loaded** when the crop really is on the truck, because it releases the farmer's advance.

## Prove delivery

Before **Mark as delivered**, fill in **Received by** with the name of the person who took the crop at the warehouse. The trip cannot close without it. The buyer sees this name on their receipt.

## Cancelling

Tap **Cancel trip** and give a reason. This works until the crop is loaded. After that, call support.

## Verification

In **Profile**, open **Verification**. Submit your driving licence, RC, insurance and permit, including the insurance and permit **expiry dates**. Staff review them, and a rejection comes with a reason so you can fix and resubmit. Expired paperwork means the truck should not carry goods, and staff can see it.

## Notifications and help

The bell on the home screen shows new updates. In **Profile**, **Help & support** raises an issue with the support team, and **Ask a question** answers questions such as who is available for delivery. See [Messages, notifications and the assistant](messages-notifications-assistant.md).
