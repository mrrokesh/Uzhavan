# Driver guide (Uzhavan)

The driver side of the app has three tabs: **Jobs**, **My Trips** and **Profile**.

## Sign in or create an account

Choose **Driver** when you create an account, then add your truck details in **Profile**: plate number, capacity, and the dates your insurance and permit expire. Expired paperwork means the vehicle should not carry goods, and staff can see it.

## Go online

On **Jobs**, switch **Online** on when you are ready for work. Only online drivers show up when a buyer is picking a truck. Switch it off when you are done.

## Take a job

Jobs are paid bookings that were routed to your truck. Open one to see the pickup farm, the destination and the load. Tap **Accept** to take it. Another driver cannot take your job.

## Run the trip

Move the trip forward one step at a time, in this order:

1. **Accepted**
2. **Reached the farm**
3. **Loaded**
4. **On the way**
5. **Delivered**

You cannot skip a step. Confirming **Loaded** is what releases the farmer's advance, so only tap it when the crop is actually on the truck.

## Prove delivery

To mark **Delivered**, enter the name of the person who received the crop at the warehouse. The trip cannot close without it. The buyer sees this name on their receipt.

## Cancelling

You can cancel before the crop is loaded, with a reason. After it is loaded, call support.

## Verification

Submit your driving licence, RC, insurance and permit in **Verification**. Staff review them. Rejections come with a reason, so you can fix and resubmit.

## Notifications and help

The bell on the home screen shows new updates. In **Profile**, **Help & support** raises an issue with the support team, and **Ask a question** answers questions like who is available for delivery. See [Messages, notifications and the assistant](messages-notifications-assistant.md).
