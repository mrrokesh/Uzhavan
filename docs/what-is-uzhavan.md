# What is Uzhavan?

Uzhavan connects three groups of people in Tamil Nadu, with no middlemen:

- **Farmers** list the crops they will harvest.
- **Buyers** (wholesale dealers) ask for a quantity and agree the price.
- **Drivers** carry the crop from the farm to the buyer's warehouse.

## How a deal works

1. The **farmer** lists a crop with a price and a quantity.
2. The **buyer** finds it, chooses how many kilograms they want, and sends a request. Nothing is reserved yet.
3. The **farmer** sets the final price and accepts, or declines and gives a reason.
4. The **buyer** confirms. An order is created and that quantity is reserved, so nobody else can take it.
5. The **buyer** pays in full. The payment is held safely until the crop arrives.
6. The **buyer** books a truck that fits the load, or uses their own.
7. The **driver** accepts the job, then marks each step: reached the farm, loaded, on the way, delivered.
8. At delivery the driver records who received the crop. Both sides can then rate each other.

## Rules that never change

- **The price is not final until the farmer says so.** Until then it is labelled "Estimated".
- **No payment before the farmer accepts.**
- **Only the right person can change a status.** A buyer cannot accept their own request, and a farmer cannot move a trip.
- **A listing cannot be oversold.** Confirmed orders reserve their quantity.
- **Delivery needs proof.** A trip cannot close without the name of the person who received the crop.
- **Farmers and drivers pay nothing.** The platform fee is added on top of the farmer's price and paid by the buyer. The farmer always receives exactly the price they accepted.
- **You can only rate someone you traded with**, once, after the crop arrived.

## Words you will see

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Listing | A crop a farmer has put up for sale |
| Request | A buyer asking for a quantity, waiting for the farmer's answer |
| Order | A confirmed request, with quantity reserved |
| Booking | A truck arranged to carry an order |
| Trip | The journey for a booking, from farm to warehouse |
| ID Verified | A free badge for accounts whose documents staff have checked |
| Escrow | Money held safely until delivery is complete |

## Who can do what

| Role | Uses | Can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer | Uzhavan Buy app | Browse, request, confirm, pay, book trucks, track, rate, chat with farmers |
| Farmer | Uzhavan app | List crops, answer requests, see demand and payouts, chat with buyers |
| Driver | Uzhavan app | Take jobs, move trips forward, record proof of delivery |
| Staff / Admin | Web console | Verify accounts, support tickets, announcements, payouts, settings |

If you sign into the wrong app, you are told which app to use instead.
